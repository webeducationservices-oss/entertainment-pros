The Great Room / Reference System

The centerpiece of the home: a true reference two-channel and cinema wall on the walnut feature wall.

The experience. A pair of MartinLogan Neolith electrostatic loudspeakers in Rosso Fuoco red, each driven by its own McIntosh MC901 dual-mono amplifier, anchor the room. The 85-inch Samsung Frame Pro displays  art when the system rests. Every source is on display: the MT5 turntable floats on a wall-mounted glass shelf, the C55 preamplifier sits below it, and the DS200 streaming DAC rides on the MPC1500 power controller in the cabinet's center bay.

The furniture. A custom low cabinet in 1.5-inch low-iron glass with open, ventilated ends. Weathered riveted-aluminum divider plates carry the load, echoing the rat-rod artwork above.
